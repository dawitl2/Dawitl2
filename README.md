## 🚀 Featured Projects

### 🌍 [GeoP](https://github.com/dawitl2/GeoP)

Globe-centered geopolitical intelligence platform for exploring countries, conflicts, economic data, global reporting, and physical geography.

**Stack:** `Next.js` · `React` · `TypeScript` · `NestJS` · `PostgreSQL` · `Prisma` · `MapLibre GL JS` · `WebGL`

---

### 📺 [Samsung Pocket Remote](https://github.com/dawitl2/Pocket_Samsung_Remote)

Native Android Samsung Smart TV remote with local Wi-Fi control, touchpad navigation, automation, voice features, and real-time hand gesture recognition.

**Stack:** `Kotlin` · `Jetpack Compose` · `MVVM` · `WebSockets` · `CameraX` · `MediaPipe` · `Room`

---

### 👁️ [TrafficVision](https://github.com/dawitl2/traffic_vision)

Local computer-vision traffic analysis system for vehicle detection, tracking, license-plate recognition, speed estimation, traffic rules, and evidence review.

**Stack:** `Python` · `FastAPI` · `OpenCV` · `YOLO` · `ByteTrack` · `ONNX Runtime` · `React` · `TypeScript`

---

### 💳 [Bank Tracker](https://github.com/dawitl2/bank-tracker)

Real-world financial tracking platform that combines bank receipts, QR scanning, OCR, analytics, and an Android SMS companion for live account updates.

**Stack:** `React` · `Node.js` · `Express` · `Supabase` · `PostgreSQL` · `Puppeteer` · `Tesseract.js` · `Android`

---

### 🎮 [AddisStrip](https://github.com/dawitl2/AddisStrip)

3D open-world Unity prototype inspired by Addis Ababa, featuring driveable vehicles, NPCs, traffic, interactive environments, and custom 3D assets.

**Stack:** `Unity` · `C#` · `Blender`
